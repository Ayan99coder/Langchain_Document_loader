# A Little Dream

I have a little dream inside my heart,
A hope that grows with every start.
Through every challenge, through every night,
I keep on walking toward the light.

The road may be long, the journey unknown,
But every small step is my own.
With courage to learn and strength to try,
I’ll chase my dreams across the sky.

Dreams are seeds that slowly grow,
With patience, effort, they start to show.
So I will learn, I will believe,
And build the future I can achieve. 🌟
